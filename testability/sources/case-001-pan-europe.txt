PASSAGE (b), statement reproduced on the page:
As already answered to you on 13 May, the Commission is aware of and shares the concerns regarding the exports to third countries of EU-banned pesticides. The Commission is committed to addressing this important issue. And, as stated in its Vision for Agriculture and Food, the Commission is assessing the issue of the export of hazardous chemicals, including pesticides, that are banned in the EU. Ensuring a high level of protection for people and the environment, both within the EU and globally, is paramount. However, a unilateral production and export ban in the EU would not guarantee an improvement in health and environmental protection in affected countries, as it may push these countries to buy the same or worse chemical pesticides from companies outside of the EU. Consequently, this would only penalise the EU chemical industry that is already suffering in the current economic climate.
